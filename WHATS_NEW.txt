What's New in v2 — a complete overhaul

winds.mobi has been fully redesigned. Same free wind data, a much better experience.

- New multi-view layout: swipe through several screens for each station
   • Now: average & max wind, direction, gust factor and data freshness
   • 1-hour history: wind & gust trend chart
   • Compass: wind direction on a compass rose
   • Stats: min / average / max and peak gust over the last hour

- Station menu: pin several spots and switch between them in one tap
- Gust factor (turbulence) indicator, colour-coded from smooth to rough
- Data freshness at a glance: colour shows how recent the reading is
- Redesigned glance: direction arrow + speed + freshness, cleaner and easier to read
- On-screen page indicator so you always know where you are
- Much wider device support: many more Garmin watches and Edge computers
- Knots / km/h and metres / feet units
- Various fixes and performance improvements

Thanks for using winds.mobi — safe flights and fair winds!
