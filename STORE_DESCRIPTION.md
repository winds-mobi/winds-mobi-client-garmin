# winds.mobi — Live Wind on Your Garmin, Free

**Know the wind before you go. Right from your wrist.**

winds.mobi brings real-time wind readings from thousands of weather stations straight to your Garmin watch and Edge computer. Built for the people who live by the wind — paraglider and hang-glider pilots, sailors, windsurfers, kitesurfers, and every outdoor adventurer who checks the sky before heading out.

Powered by the [winds.mobi](https://winds.mobi) network, it turns your device into a pocket weather station — **completely free, no ads, no subscription.**

---

## Why you'll love it

- **Real-time wind, everywhere** — accurate wind speed and direction from stations worldwide, updated continuously.
- **Find stations near you** — let GPS surface the closest beacons for instant local conditions.
- **See the full picture** — swipe through multiple views for each station:
  - **Now** — average & max wind, direction, gust factor and data freshness at a glance
  - **1-hour history** — a clean wind & gust trend chart
  - **Compass** — wind direction on a compass rose
  - **Stats** — min / average / max and peak gust over the last hour
- **Turbulence indicator** — the gust factor tells you how gusty the air really is, colour-coded from smooth to rough.
- **Your stations, your way** — pin your favourite spots, then jump between them from the station menu.
- **Your units** — km/h or knots, metres or feet.
- **Wide compatibility** — runs on a large range of Garmin watches *and* Edge cycling computers.
- **Free, forever** — every feature, no cost.

---

## Getting started

1. Open the app **settings** on your device or in Garmin Connect.
2. Enter the **station ID(s)** you want to follow.
3. That's it — your wind data appears instantly.

> **Where do I find a station ID?** It's the code at the end of the station's page URL on the [winds.mobi](https://winds.mobi) website (see screenshot).

Prefer automatic? Enable **GPS** in the settings and the app will show the nearest stations around you.
